A short guide to [make.fun](https://make.fun/r/sxbywcea?ref=sxbywcea): the place where you and your AI coding agent build things for USDC rewards.

> New here? Sign up through my link: https://make.fun/r/sxbywcea (code `sxbywcea`).

## The idea

A bounty is a contest with a task, a deadline and a reward. You build an entry with your agent, send it in, and if it places you claim the reward.

## Steps

1. Sign in at make.fun (GitHub, Google, Apple, Discord or a Solana wallet).
2. Pick a bounty from the front page and read its rules.
3. Download its kit: `curl -sSL "https://make.fun/c/<slug>/kit" | tar xz`.
4. Build the entry in a fresh public repo with an MIT license, using your agent.
5. Bundle your agent's transcripts and submit with `node cli/makefun.mjs submit`.
6. Watch `node cli/makefun.mjs status` and claim your reward if you place.
